# Music Credits

Attribution for third-party music shipped with this game.

**These credits must be reachable by the player** — credits screen, README, or
store page. Every track below is Creative Commons Attribution or an equivalent
credit-required grant, so shipping the audio without these credits makes the
build non-compliant. This is a licence condition, not a courtesy.

Licences verified 2026-07-30. The build is assumed **commercial**, so every
track here permits commercial use; NonCommercial (NC) and ShareAlike (SA)
material is deliberately excluded.

## Per-track sources and caveats

### Alexander Nakarada — Chase
CC BY 4.0. Commercial use permitted with attribution. Downloaded from the
artist's own site, <https://creatorchords.com/music/chase/>. An
attribution-free paid licence (~$20 personal tier) is available if you would
rather not carry the credit.

### Ross Bugden — Dragon Slayer
Not a formal CC licence — a bespoke grant stated on the artist's own channel:
*"free to use and monetize. No need to get permission. Just credit me."*
Downloaded from the download link in that same description.
Source: <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BnzKZw4lquQ>

> **Content ID caveat — the one real snag in this set.** Bugden runs Content ID
> through Symphonic Distribution to deter fraudulent claims. This does not
> restrict use in the game itself, but any YouTube video containing the track
> will draw an automated claim — your own trailers, and *streamers' and Let's
> Players' footage of your game*. His stated remedy is to dispute with "I have
> properly credited Ross Bugden", released within 30 days. Consider whether
> that friction is acceptable for a track that plays during combat, i.e. the
> most-recorded part of the game. The other four combat tracks have no such
> issue, so dropping this one costs little if it becomes a nuisance.

### Scott Buckley — Juggernaut, March of Midnight, Permafrost, The Long Dark
CC BY 4.0 across his whole library, with *"Software – games, apps"* named
explicitly as a permitted use. Direct downloads from
<https://www.scottbuckley.com.au/library/>. Paid attribution-free licences
(AU$40–300/track) exist if you ever need to drop the credit.

### Kevin MacLeod — Celtic Impulse
CC BY 4.0. Commercial and game use permitted with attribution. Downloaded from
<https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/>.

### Meydän — Surreal Forest
CC BY 3.0, from the album *Hávor*. Every Meydän album on the Free Music Archive
carries a single uniform CC BY licence — unusually clean, no per-track
checking needed. Obtained from the Internet Archive mirror of the Jamendo
release, which records the licence URL in item metadata:
<https://archive.org/details/jamendo-180311>

## Rejected — do not add these back

### Dmitri Shostakovich — String Quartet No. 8
**Not usable.** Composed 1960; Shostakovich died 1975. Two separate rights
layers are both still live:
- *The composition* — under copyright until roughly 2045 in life+70 territories
  (EU, UK), and later still in the US, where foreign works of this vintage were
  restored to a 95-years-from-publication term. Publisher of record: G. Schirmer
  o/b/o VAAP.
- *Any recording* — a separately owned master, independent of the composition.

A public-domain or CC-licensed *performance* would not help, because the
composition is the restricted layer. Replaced by **Juggernaut** (Scott
Buckley), chosen for its chugging, malevolent string writing — the closest
available analogue to the quartet's grinding texture.

### The Maid at the Spinning Wheel — traditional
**Needs a specific recording, and none was cleared.** The *tune* is a
traditional Irish jig and is firmly public domain, so no composition rights are
owed — but every recorded performance carries its own master copyright, and
there are 65+ known recordings. "The tune is public domain" does not make any
particular recording free to use. Replaced by **Celtic Impulse** (Kevin
MacLeod).

If you specifically want this tune, the clean routes are to commission or
record a performance yourself, or to find one explicitly released CC BY / CC0.
[The Session](https://thesession.org/tunes/2254) catalogues the recordings.

### Kai Engel — Snowfall, Soli, Cold War Echo
**Dropped — licensing is too muddy for a commercial build.** Three separate
problems:

1. **Most of the catalogue is NonCommercial.** Verified licence URLs:

   | Release | Licence |
   |---|---|
   | Chapter One / Cold (contains *Snowfall*) | CC BY-**NC** 3.0 |
   | Irsen's Tale, The Scope, Written in Ink, Idea, Atlantida, Rain Catcher | CC BY-**NC** |
   | Calls and Echoes, Paradigm Lost, Evening Colors, Deathless | CC BY-**NC-SA** |
   | The Run | CC BY 3.0 |

   *Snowfall* is NC, so it cannot ship in a commercial game. NC-SA is worse
   still — ShareAlike is actively hostile to a game project.

2. **Conflicting licence claims on the two tracks that looked usable.** The
   Free Music Archive lists *Cold War Echo* as Attribution, but the artist's own
   Bandcamp page for the same track shows **all rights reserved**. *Soli*
   (album *Caeli*) has no confirmable licence at all. When the artist's own
   storefront contradicts the aggregator, that is not a conflict worth
   inheriting.

3. **The artist has withdrawn from Creative Commons** for work released after
   1 July 2025 and now licenses directly (<https://www.kai-engel.com/>).
   Pre-existing CC grants remain valid — CC licences are irrevocable — but the
   direction of travel plus the contradiction above makes this a poor bet for a
   commercial release.

Replaced by **Permafrost** and **The Long Dark** (Scott Buckley) and **Surreal
Forest** (Meydän), which cover the same cold, sparse, melancholy register with
unambiguous commercial licensing.

> If you specifically want *Snowfall*, Kai Engel accepts direct licensing
> enquiries at Contact@Kai-Engel.com. That is the clean route.
